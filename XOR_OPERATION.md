# XOR Operation in Calculator-MJ

## What is XOR?

XOR (exclusive OR) is a bitwise operation that compares two binary numbers bit by bit. It returns 1 when the bits are different and 0 when they are the same.

## How it works in the calculator

The calculator converts floating-point numbers to integers before performing the XOR operation, then converts the result back to a float for display.

## Examples

- 5 XOR 3 = 6
  - Binary: 101 XOR 011 = 110
  - Decimal: 5 XOR 3 = 6

- 8 XOR 4 = 12
  - Binary: 1000 XOR 0100 = 1100
  - Decimal: 8 XOR 4 = 12

- 15 XOR 7 = 8
  - Binary: 1111 XOR 0111 = 1000
  - Decimal: 15 XOR 7 = 8

## Usage

1. Enter the first number
2. Press the "xor" button
3. Enter the second number
4. Press "=" to see the result
